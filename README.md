### About Me

I am a senior full-stack developer with over 13 years of practical experience building, deploying, and maintaining web applications, secure media delivery platforms, and digital products. Over the years, I have worked on SaaS tools, e-commerce systems with custom admin panels, mobile applications, and business platforms for teams and clients worldwide.

I handle end-to-end delivery across the entire stack:

- **Backend & APIs:** PHP, Laravel, Python, Django, REST APIs, and third-party webhook integrations.
- **Frontend & UI:** Next.js, Astro, React, JavaScript, jQuery, AJAX, Tailwind CSS, and Bootstrap.
- **Data & Databases:** MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, SQLite, plus structured JSON and XML data feeds.
- **Cloud & Infrastructure:** AWS (EC2, S3, RDS, SES, Elastic Transcoder), Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, cPanel, GoDaddy, and Bluehost.
- **Gateways & External Services:**

  - **SMS & Communications:** Twilio, Dove SMS.
  - **Email, CRM & Platforms:** Brevo, Zoho, Google API (OAuth, Maps, Drive), Facebook API, LinkedIn API.
- **Mobile & Protected Media:** Android application development, eClass education apps, local SQLite storage, and video encryption to protect streaming media from unauthorized ripping and piracy.
- **E-Commerce & Dashboards:** Custom administrative dashboards, inventory control, and order handling pipelines.

### Security, Data & Mobile Media Protection

- **Safe Queries & Forms:** 100% parameterized queries and ORM handling to prevent SQL injection, plus XSS sanitization and CSRF verification across forms and APIs.
- **Data Formats & Local Databases:** Strong experience parsing, generating, and transforming structured JSON and XML feeds, alongside lightweight local storage using SQLite.
- **Secure Webhooks & APIs:** Cryptographic signature verification for payment and logistics webhooks, token authentication, and strict input validation.
- **Mobile Video Encryption (Android):** Implemented AES-based video encryption and secure media playback pipelines to protect course and educational content from piracy, screen scraping, and direct file ripping.
- **eClass Educational Apps:** Built native Android applications for educational platforms, supporting offline SQLite caching, authenticated student sessions, and push notifications.
- **Server Safety:** Strict file upload validation (checking actual MIME types, not just extensions) and environment secret protection.
